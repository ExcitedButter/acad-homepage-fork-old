Zhi Cao曹智

Robot Learning · World Models · Video Generation

I am a researcher at the Stanford Vision and Learning Lab, working with Prof. Fei-Fei Li and Prof. Jiajun Wu. I studied Computer Science at the University of Michigan, where I worked with Prof. Jason J. Corso on hand–object video generation.

My research asks how robots can learn dexterous, multi-step manipulation from the abundance of human and video data. I build models that connect perception, prediction and action: visual-track world models that bridge video prediction and robot control, multimodal force-action foundation models for human-to-robot transfer, agent-friendly simulation for real-to-sim-to-real manipulation, and controllable video generation of hands interacting with objects.
