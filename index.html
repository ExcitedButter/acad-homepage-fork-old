Zhi Cao

I am a researcher at Stanford University, fortunately advised by Dr. Huang Huang, Prof. Jiajun Wu, and Prof. Fei-Fei Li. Previously, I studied Computer Science at the University of Michigan, where I was advised by Prof. Jason J. Corso and Prof. Alexander Rodríguez.

My research focuses on robot learning from human and video data, especially dexterous manipulation, world models, and real-to-sim-to-real transfer. I build models that connect perception, prediction, and action: multimodal force-action foundation models for human-to-robot dexterity, visual-track world models that bridge video prediction and robot control, and agent-friendly simulation for manipulation.

Selected Publications

* Equal contribution  ·  † Equal advising
FACT: A Multimodal Force-Action-Track Foundation Model for Human-to-Robot Dexterity
Zhi Cao, Yining Hong, Jiaolong Yang, Yejin Choi, Li Fei-Fei, Jiajun Wu, Huang Huang
arXiv 2026 · Website · Paper

FACT jointly models force, action, and visual tracks to transfer dexterous skills from human demonstrations to robot hands, enabling contact-rich tasks such as playing the harp.
TrAct: Bridging Robot Control and Visual Prediction with Visual Tracks
Zhi Cao*, Howard Ji*, Kevin Zhang*, Kuangzhi Ge, Li Fei-Fei, Jiajun Wu†, Huang Huang†
TAG 2026 · Oral · Website · Paper

TrAct uses visual tracks as a shared interface between robot control and video prediction: it proposes action-track pairs, predicts their visual outcomes, and selects actions with a vision-language reward model.
Cleave: An Agent-Friendly Simulator with Schema-Based Agents for Real-to-Sim-to-Real Manipulation
Zhi Cao, Zesen Zhao, Zixuan Lu, Yining Hong, Peihao Li, Yin Yang, Zhuoqing Mao, Chenfanfu Jiang, Li Fei-Fei, Jiajun Wu, Huang Huang
Under review

An agent-friendly simulator in which schema-based agents turn human demonstrations into simulated robot executions for deformable and contact-rich manipulation, such as tying shoelaces.
T²Mem: Learning Test-Time Memory for Robotics
Yize Liu, Huang Huang, Yining Hong, Zijian Du, Zhi Cao, Li Fei-Fei, Jiajun Wu
arXiv 2026 · Website · Paper

T²Mem equips robot policies with a memory that is learned and updated at test time, letting them solve tasks that depend on what happened earlier in an episode.
BiMotion: B-spline Motion for Text-guided Dynamic 3D Character Generation
Miaowei Wang, Qingxuan Yan, Zhi Cao, Yayuan Li, Oisin Mac Aodha, Jason J. Corso, Amir Vaxman
CVPR 2026 · Highlight · Website · Paper

BiMotion represents character motion with B-spline curves, producing smooth and expressive text-guided dynamic 3D characters.
HANDI: Hand-Centric Text-and-Image Conditioned Video Generation
Yayuan Li, Zhi Cao, Jason J. Corso
ICCV 2025 Workshop (HANDS) · Website · Paper

HANDI generates videos with detailed hand appearance and motion from an image and an action description, using an automatically inferred motion area and a hand refinement loss.
